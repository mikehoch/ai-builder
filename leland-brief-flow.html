Sub-build 1  ·  Inbox Scraper
🌐
Browser Automation
Claude in Chrome navigates joinleland.com while logged in
Est. 1–2 hrs to build
1
Navigate to inbox
Open joinleland.com/inbox, take screenshot, confirm logged-in state and page structure
2
Read thread list
Scan the visible inbox for all message threads — capture contact name and preview for each
3
Filter to last 7 days
Skip threads with no activity in the past 7 days — only scrape recent, actionable conversations
4
Click into each thread
Open each qualifying thread, scroll to capture full message history, extract the complete back-and-forth
5
Return raw structured data
For each thread: contact name · message timestamps · full message text in chronological order

Sub-build 2  ·  Classifier + Brief Generator
🔀
Contact Classification
Each contact is assigned a type based on relationship and conversation context
Est. 1 hr to build
1
Read thread context
Analyze message history — first message sent, tone, what's been discussed, what's outstanding
2
Assign contact type
Classify each contact into one of three categories based on relationship stage
New Lead
First contact, intro call scheduled or pending
Confirm upcoming call · Ask for resume and target school list if not provided
Confirm & qualify
Warm Lead
Intro call completed, considering coaching
Follow up with recommended next steps · Present coaching package options
Convert
Active Client
Paying client in an active engagement
Respond to questions or logistics · Address agenda items and scheduling
Respond
🚦
Urgency Scoring
Every contact gets a status — determines position in the brief
Urgent
Active client who needs a response · OR warm lead that can convert to a paid client now
Recommend Reply
Warm lead following up · New lead with a routine question · Routine client logistics
No Reply Needed
Thread is acknowledged and current · No open question or pending action
Classified threads — [type, urgency, context]
✍️
Brief Generation
Structured prompt produces summaries and draft replies per contact
1
Summarize context per contact
2–3 sentences capturing what's been discussed, what's pending, and what Mike needs to know before replying
2
Draft reply for every thread needing one
Tone-matched to contact type — confirm/qualify for new leads, conversion-focused for warm leads, responsive and practical for active clients
3
Assemble the brief
Order by urgency (Urgent first), then structure into three sections below
Formatted brief — ready to read in 5 minutes
Output  ·  Three-Section Brief
Section 1
Priority Action List
All contacts requiring follow-up, ordered by urgency, with a 3–5 word action label for each.
Jordan T. — confirm meeting
Sarah K. — recommend package
Alex M. — answer essay Q
Section 2
Contact Summaries + Drafts
For each contact: context summary, then a draft reply to copy and edit. Only shown for threads needing a reply.
[Context] Jordan booked a call for…
[Draft] Hi Jordan, looking forward…
Section 3
Full Status Table
All contacts active in the last 7 days — name, last contact date, and status (Urgent / Recommend reply / No reply needed).
Jordan T. · May 3 · Urgent
Sarah K. · May 2 · Recommend
Alex M. · May 1 · No reply
